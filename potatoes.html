Garlic Mashed Potatoes

  • Prep Time: 10 mins
  • Cook Time: 15 mins
  • Total Time: 25 mins
  • Servings: 6

Mashed Potato Ingredients

  • Potatoes: Any kind will do
  • Milk and Butter
  • Garlic: Five cloves
  • Seasonings: Salt and Ground white pepper

How to make the Mashed Potatoes

Simply boil and drain the potatoes and garlic, then mash or beat them with the remaining ingredients. That’s all there is to it! Find the step-by-step recipe below for detailed instructions.

  • Adding the butter first to the hot potatoes allows you to use less milk/cream and keeps the potatoes creamier for longer.
  • Cooking the potatoes with garlic allows the garlic flavor to permeate the potatoes and sweetens the garlic’s flavor rather than using raw, harsh garlic at the end.
  • You can use russet potatoes, but they will not be as creamy as they are starchier.
  • Stir in some sour cream and chives to add extra creaminess and flavor!
