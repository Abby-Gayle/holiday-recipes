Brown Sugar Ham

This is a good one I eat every thanksgiving

  • Prep Time: 10 mins
  • Cook Time: 4hrs 30 mins
  • Additional Time: 20 hrs
  • Total Time: 5 hrs
  • Servings: 18

Ingredients

  • 12 Pound Ham
  • Whole Cloves
  • Brown Sugar
  • 1 inch of Water

How to make the Ham

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees
  2. Press cloves into the top of the ham. Pack with brown sugar.
  3. Add water to the roasting pan
  4. Bake in the preheated oven until the ham is done

You will know it is done by having an instant-read thermometer inserted into the center (not touching the bone) should read at least 160 degrees F. Tent with foil during baking if the sugar begins to darken too quickly.
